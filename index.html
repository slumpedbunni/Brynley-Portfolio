About me

Science feels most meaningful when it has a place attached to it.

A watershed, a prairie, a wetland, a neighborhood. I am most interested in work where data can be connected back to a real landscape and the people depending on it.

I earned my Associate of Science in General Science while I was still in high school, then completed my Bachelor of Science in Environmental Science at nineteen. Along the way, I built experience in GIS, Python, remote sensing, field sampling, hydrology, ecological restoration, and science communication.

I am especially drawn to conservation, restoration ecology, water resources, environmental justice, and research that can support better decisions. I bring curiosity, reliable follow-through, and the ability to explain technical work without making people feel like they need a dictionary.

My favorite work usually involves some combination of maps, field gear, a messy dataset, and the moment when the pattern finally makes sense.

Projects

Research, restoration, and environmental data with a point.

Equal parts analysis, curiosity, and staring at a map until it tells me what is wrong.

PROJECT 01

Colorado Reservoir Storage Modeling

Collaborated with an interdisciplinary research team through ESIIL STARS to model reservoir storage using streamflow, snowpack, temperature, and antecedent storage. Built and evaluated regression models, created maps and visualizations, and translated model performance into clear evidence for a nontechnical audience.

Python Hydrology Regression GIS Science Communication
PROJECT 02

Wetland and Water Quality Research

Explored how constructed wetlands and phytoremediation can improve water quality, reduce pollutant loads, and support environmental justice goals. Connected ecological mechanisms with practical restoration recommendations and community outcomes.

Restoration Ecology Water Quality Phytoremediation Environmental Justice
PROJECT 03

Landscape Ecology and Restoration Planning

Used GIS, land-cover analysis, remote sensing, and ecological context to examine habitat connectivity, urban expansion, and restoration opportunities across Colorado landscapes. Focused on making spatial analysis useful for conservation and planning.

ArcGIS Pro Remote Sensing NDVI Landscape Ecology
PROJECT 04

Community Sustainability and Environmental Education

Supported community-centered sustainability work through the Consumption Literacy Project, including garden-based learning and environmental education. Helped connect environmental ideas with everyday choices, local food systems, and accessible public engagement.

Community Engagement Environmental Education Sustainability
