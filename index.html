a little about me

About me

Hi, I’m Brynley. I ask a lot of questions, which has worked out pretty well because that is basically research.

I am most interested in work where data connects back to an actual place, whether that is a watershed, a wetland, a prairie, or a neighborhood.

I earned my Associate of Science in General Science while I was still in high school, then completed my Bachelor of Science in Environmental Science at nineteen. It still sounds a little strange when I say it out loud, but it is true.

I am especially interested in conservation, restoration ecology, water resources, environmental justice, and research that supports better decisions. I learn quickly, notice when something looks off, and explain technical work without making people wish they had never asked.

My favorite projects usually involve a map, a messy dataset, and at least one moment where the pattern finally makes sense.
